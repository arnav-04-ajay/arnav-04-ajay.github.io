Engineering Design Lab Assistant

Duke POD Design Lab

August 2023 - Present

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

Project Assistance

Help undergraduate engineering students with their design projects (both class and personal projects) and operate tools and machinary (3D printers, machine saws, lazer cutters) to assist with these projects.

Space Improvement

Perform tasks to improve the lab and create systems to ensure it remains well-stocked and organized at all times.

Long-term Lab Projects

Work on long term projects to make the space more technologically advanced and better showcase the design lab in general.

Key Deliverables

Student Occupancy Tracker

In the process of designing a system (using some computer vision on a Raspberry Pi) to estimate the active student count within the space, thus being able to better track and display metrics of space usage.

Key Skills Lessons

In the process of making lesson plans for basic engineering skills for project work, such as soldering, embedded programming, etc.

Key Highlights

Improved workflow efficiency

During the fall semester (highest level of traffic due to classes being conducted in the space), a significant improvement in space cleanliness and organization was seen. Additionally, clear systems for grading and tool usage were put into place and yielded great results.

New features integrated

Many quality of life projects were integrated into the space, such as an easy inventory tracking system, automatic lights above soldering stations, new tool stations, etc. that made the design lab more advanced.
